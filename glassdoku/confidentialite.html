Privacy Policy — Glassdoku

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Publisher: Doudimo Studio — doudimo.studio@gmail.com

In short

Glassdoku is a free logic game funded by advertising. The studio collects nothing and has no server. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which processes some data from your device to show and measure them, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (levels, records, streaks, hints, lanterns), your settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and whether you bought “No ads”. Nothing is sent to the studio. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.
  • No account, no sign-in, no name, no email. The game is fully playable offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The app shows full-screen ads between some levels and, only when you ask for one, rewarded videos (a hint, a lantern, a life).
  • To do so, Google AdMob may collect and process: your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise them.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown at first launch. You can change your choice at any time from the home screen: “…” menu → Privacy. Elsewhere, ads follow local law and your device settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional “No ads” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under its own policies. The app never sees or stores any payment data.

Leaderboards

If leaderboards are available and you use them, your scores and your Google Play Games profile name are handled by Google Play Games under Google's policies.

Age

Glassdoku is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Contact

Any question or request: doudimo.studio@gmail.com

  • Du kannst deine Werbe-ID in den Einstellungen des Telefons zurücksetzen oder löschen oder personalisierte Werbung abwählen (Google → Anzeigen).
